Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a ransomware attack known as WannaCry spread across computer systems worldwide. The malware encrypted files and demanded ransom payments to restore access. The attack disrupted hospitals, businesses, and government organizations, showing how dangerous outdated software and unpatched security vulnerabilities can be.

Types of Malware

  1. Ransomware: Locks or encrypts files and demands payment.
  2. Spyware: Secretly collects information from a device.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial gain through stealing money or sensitive data.
  2. Revenge against individuals, businesses, or organizations.

Prevention Strategies

  • Keep software updated to fix security vulnerabilities.
  • Use strong passwords and multifactor authentication.
